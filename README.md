# HireShield AI
### Detect Fake Jobs. Protect Real Careers.

## 1. Project Overview
HireShield AI is an AI-powered recruitment fraud detection platform designed to identify suspicious job postings, recruitment messages, and company websites. It analyses submitted information and provides an explainable risk score with relevant warnings and safety recommendations.

## 2. Problem Statement
Fake job postings and recruitment scams are increasingly targeting job seekers, especially students and fresh graduates. Fraudsters use unrealistic salary promises, registration fees, fake company identities, and requests for sensitive personal information to deceive candidates.

Job seekers need a reliable way to evaluate recruitment opportunities before sharing personal information or making payments.

## 3. Objectives
- Detect suspicious job postings and recruitment messages.
- Identify common recruitment fraud indicators.
- Analyse company and recruitment website URLs.
- Generate explainable risk scores.
- Provide actionable safety recommendations.
- Help users make informed decisions about job opportunities.

## 4. Key Features

### 4.1 Job Description Analysis
Allow users to submit job descriptions, recruitment messages, and website URLs for analysis.

### 4.2 AI Scam Detection
Identify suspicious patterns such as registration fees, unrealistic salaries, urgent joining requests, and sensitive information requests.

### 4.3 Website and Company Verification
Analyse recruitment URLs, domain information, and available company details to identify suspicious signals.

### 4.4 Explainable Risk Scoring
Generate a risk score between 0 and 100, accompanied by detected warning signs and explanations.

### 4.5 Community Reporting
Allow users to report suspicious recruitment opportunities and access previously reported cases.

### 4.6 User Dashboard
Provide access to previous analyses, risk scores, warnings, and community reports.
